Definitions & methodology

CFP berth probability is a transparent Calcutta planning estimate — not a betting-market or committee forecast. It uses current AP position/votes, overall record, conference record, and a 1–5 remaining-schedule risk factor. Notre Dame and Boise receive model adjustments for the special paths described in the auction rules.

Team Model EV means estimated gross payout value from the modeled probability of reaching each CFP round, before subtracting auction cost. Owner EV multiplies that by the participant's ownership share. Multi-owner bids are split equally unless explicitly corrected; Georgia is set to Dagem/John 50/50.

Any CFP cash estimates the chance that at least one known team in that owner's portfolio earns a playoff payout. The Field is treated as an aggregate lot and cannot be simulated like a single team.
