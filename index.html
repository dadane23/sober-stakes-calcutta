Definitions & methodology

ESPN FPI playoff probability is ESPN's published chance that a team makes the College Football Playoff. ESPN says its projections use 20,000 simulations of the rest of the season based on FPI, results to date and the remaining schedule. The Field is an auction-specific bundle, so its displayed probability combines the individual ESPN playoff chances for every FPI team outside the 35 named lots using an independence approximation.

Team Model EV means estimated gross payout value from the modeled probability of reaching each CFP round, before subtracting auction cost. Owner EV multiplies that by the participant's ownership share. Ownership percentages follow the updated results workbook; entries using a double slash assign 50% to the first owner and split the remaining 50% among the other listed owners.

Probability of at least one CFP payout estimates the chance that at least one known team in that participant's portfolio earns a playoff payout. Weekly movement compares the participant's current EV rank with the most recent prior Monday snapshot. The Field is treated as an aggregate lot and cannot be simulated like a single team.
